## Blocker on pre-filling ATS forms (found 2026-09-08)

The public/guest capture path **cannot resolve an offsite apply URL**. On every posting
checked, the `apply-link-offsite` anchor on `jobs-guest/jobs/api/jobPosting/<id>` points at
`linkedin.com/signup/cold-join?...contextual-sign-in-modal`, not at the employer's ATS —
LinkedIn requires a session before it hands out the destination. There is also no
`~/.linkedin-chrome` session on this machine.

Searching employer career sites by title is not a substitute: Stripe's site surfaced
"Staff Engineer, Release Engineering" and several adjacent Staff SWE roles but not the
Deployment Platform requisition, and applying to the wrong requisition is worse than not
applying.

**To get forms pre-filled:** click Apply on an open LinkedIn tab (you are logged in there),
let it land on the real ATS, and paste that URL back. Then `describeForm()` →
`uploadThenSettle()` → fill → `auditAttestations()` runs as normal.

Note that GEICO and Early Warning both run Workday, which the ATS playbook records as
account-walled — those two are likely a stop regardless of URL.
