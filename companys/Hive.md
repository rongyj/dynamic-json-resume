## 1. Biggest Leadership Lesson

My biggest leadership lesson is that empowering others is the key to building high-performing, resilient teams.

Early in my career, I believed that strong leadership meant having all the answers and driving every decision. Over time, I learned that the most effective leaders create an environment where team members feel trusted, valued, and empowered to take ownership. This shift in mindset transformed my approach: I began focusing on setting clear expectations, providing context and support, and then stepping back to let people bring their own ideas and solutions forward.

One pivotal moment was when I led a cross-functional team through a high-stakes cloud migration project. Initially, I tried to tightly manage every detail, but progress stalled and morale dipped. Realizing my approach was stifling initiative, I started delegating more, encouraging open dialogue, and inviting team members to lead key workstreams. The results were immediate: engagement soared, creative solutions emerged, and we delivered ahead of schedule.

This experience taught me that my role as a leader is not to be the smartest person in the room, but to create the conditions for others to excel. By fostering psychological safety, encouraging diverse perspectives, and trusting my team, I’ve seen people grow beyond their own expectations—and deliver results that exceed mine.

Ultimately, leadership is about enabling others to do their best work. When you invest in people, give them autonomy, and support their growth, you build teams that are not only more effective, but also more fulfilled and resilient in the face of change.
