## Summary

JSI is a full-service consulting firm specializing in broadband and telecommunications solutions. The Software Architect will establish and implement the architecture, frameworks, standards, and reusable components for AI-enabled products, internal tools, and SaaS solutions while hands-on developing applications from prototype through production. The role also provides technical leadership across software development, security, cloud infrastructure, DevOps, and AI application development.
