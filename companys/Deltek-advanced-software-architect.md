## Summary

Deltek delivers software and information solutions for project-based businesses. The Advanced Software Architect will lead the design and development of scalable UI, AI, and services platform architectures, while guiding technical decisions, integrating AI capabilities, reviewing code, and collaborating with product and engineering teams.

## Core responsibilities

- Collaborate with product managers, UX designers and engineering leaders to understand project requirements and business objectives
- Design and develop a robust component architecture for the designer tool, creating reusable, modular UI components that adhere to best practices in JavaScript development. Ensure these components are optimized for performance and can be easily integrated and extended by other team members
- Additional key responsibilities include designing system architecture, code review, technical documentation, and collaborating with product teams to align technical solutions with business requirements
- Provide technical leadership and guidance to product teams, ensuring that architectural decisions are well-understood and implemented
- Share ownership of roadmap execution, architecture decisions, and day-to-day technical operations related to the AI and Services platform
- Act as a key technical liaison between the AI and Services platform team and product stakeholders — translating business and product requirements into scalable technical designs, and clearly communicating technical tradeoffs back to non-technical stakeholders
- Guide the framework and services/microservices architecture underlying the AI and Services platform, ensuring services are well-decomposed, resilient, and built for extensibility as new AI-driven capabilities are added
- Partner with AI/ML engineers and product teams to integrate AI and agentic capabilities (e.g., orchestration, natural-language interfaces, connector/MCP-style integrations) into the platform, evaluating emerging AI tooling and frameworks for fit
- Create and maintain documentation related to the software architecture, including system diagrams, design specifications, and best practices
- Review code and provide feedback to ensure it adheres to the architectural standards, performance requirements, and coding best practices
- Stay up-to-date on emerging technologies and industry trends — particularly in AI/agentic systems and microservices — to continuously improve software architecture and tools
- Contribute to product decisions and help drive technical innovation within the team
