## Summary

Upstart is an AI lending marketplace that uses advanced technology to expand access to affordable credit. The Principal Software Engineer will provide cross-company technical leadership for the pricing platform, shaping its architecture and roadmap while leading the development of scalable, low-latency, highly reliable pricing services across multiple loan products. The role partners with engineering, machine learning, product, and business teams and mentors senior technical leaders.

## Core responsibilities

- Define the cross-company technical vision and roadmap for Upstart's pricing platform, ensuring alignment across Pricing, Personal Loans, Auto, HELOC, ML, and Product organizations
- Lead the architectural evolution from tightly coupled, sequential workflows toward modular, reusable platform capabilities across loan products
- Design and improve Tier 0/Tier 1 services where latency, throughput, availability, and financial correctness are all critical
- Partner closely with machine-learning scientists and engineers to create reliable, scalable boundaries between models and production pricing systems
- Balance the sometimes-competing needs of engineering, machine learning, and product teams across organizations, making pragmatic trade-offs that serve Upstart's broader technical and business strategy
- Clarify service, API, configuration, and ownership boundaries across Pricing, Personal Loans, Auto, HELOC, and adjacent platform teams
- Evaluate runtime and language choices pragmatically, including where Python, Kotlin, Rust or Go best serve long-term system needs
- Reduce operational burden through better testing, observability, failure isolation, incident learning, and architecture that limits blast radius
- Lead company-wide technical discussions, surface trade-offs clearly, and build alignment across Engineering, ML, Product, Data, Security, Compliance, and business partners
- Stay close to implementation through design reviews, prototypes, and critical code while mentoring senior technical leaders and making the people around you more successful
- Prioritize technical-debt and platform investments based on business value, risk reduction, and long-term return rather than technical elegance alone
