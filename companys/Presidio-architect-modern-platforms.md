## Summary

Presidio is a technology solutions company delivering digital solutions, cloud services, cybersecurity, automation, and AI-driven analytics. The Cloud Infrastructure Architect designs, builds, and optimizes secure, scalable cloud-native infrastructure across enterprise environments, leading cloud migration, platform engineering, governance, reliability, and cost optimization initiatives. The role also advises stakeholders and mentors engineering teams on cloud adoption and best practices.

## Core responsibilities

- Design and document highly available, secure, and performant cloud-native architectures across AWS (preferred), Azure, and GCP
- Define engineering standards and reusable patterns for compute, networking, storage, and platform services (e.g., containers, serverless, messaging, databases)
- Integrate Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using tools like Terraform, ARM templates, or CloudFormation to build repeatable deployments
- Establish and implement cloud adoption frameworks and landing zone strategies (e.g., AWS Landing Zones, Azure Landing Zones, GCP Foundations) to ensure consistent, governed, and scalable cloud foundations across enterprise environments
- Lead and architect end-to-end cloud migration initiatives, encompassing assessment, planning, and execution of lift-and-shift, replatforming, and application modernization strategies
- Evaluate existing on-premises and hybrid workloads to define migration roadmaps aligned with business priorities, risk tolerance, and target architectures
- Apply proven migration frameworks and patterns (e.g., AWS Migration Acceleration Program, Azure Migrate, Google Cloud Migration Framework) to drive structured, low-disruption transitions
- Collaborate with application, infrastructure, and security teams to validate migration readiness and ensure continuity of operations throughout the migration lifecycle
- Lead the implementation of CI/CD pipelines and automated infrastructure deployments
- Engineer resilient, auto-scaling solutions using cloud-native tools (e.g., AWS ECS/Fargate, Azure Kubernetes Service, Google Cloud Run)
- Implement monitoring, alerting, and observability frameworks (e.g., CloudWatch, Azure Monitor, GCP Operations Suite)
- Enforce cloud governance policies for tagging, resource usage, budget thresholds, and identity management
- Architect secure environments using IAM, encryption, key management, and network security controls
- Support regulatory and compliance requirements (HIPAA, FedRAMP, ISO 27001) through secure design patterns and audit readiness
- Continuously evaluate performance and cost efficiency of deployed cloud resources
- Optimize workloads using rightsizing, reserved instances, and modern architectural strategies (e.g., event-driven, microservices)
- Conduct architecture reviews and well-architected assessments to recommend improvements
- Apply FinOps principles and tooling (e.g., AWS Cost Explorer, Azure Cost Management, CloudHealth, Apptio Cloudability) to establish financial accountability, drive cost transparency, and optimize cloud spend across business units
- Support SRE principles by embedding resilience, fault-tolerance, and operational visibility in all solutions
- Collaborate with DevOps and infrastructure teams to implement monitoring, logging, and alerting at scale
- Troubleshoot systemic issues in cloud environments and drive root cause resolution
- Serve as a trusted advisor to business and technology leaders, leading discovery and design sessions that translate complex requirements into actionable cloud infrastructure strategies
- Present architecture recommendations, roadmaps, and solution outcomes to executive and senior stakeholder audiences, communicating technical concepts with clarity and business context
- Drive cloud adoption strategy across client organizations, influencing decision-making at the architectural and investment levels
- Guide and mentor engineering and development teams on cloud-native best practices, fostering a culture of technical excellence and continuous improvement
- Champion knowledge sharing through documentation, internal communities of practice, and cross-functional enablement sessions
