You will lead the creation of complex design documents, present architectural strategy to senior technical and cross-functional audiences, and guide the implementation of solutions across the team. We are developing the next-gen architecture for FKP, and as a PMTS you will be a primary architect of this transformation. You will design and implement distributed systems, control planes, and APIs that operate at planet scale, orchestrating and managing clusters and resources across thousands of clusters and all substrates. You will identify architectural gaps, propose and drive solutions, and mentor other engineers as they build against your designs. Desired Skills And Experience Deep expertise in the following programming languages or equivalents: Golang Bash (working shell knowledge required) Deep, Hands-on Experience With The Following Technologies 8+ years experience with distributed systems, APIs, Kubernetes, Docker, and container orchestration at scale 5+ years experience with AWS and/or GCP services Strong experience with Terraform and infrastructure-as-code practices CI/CD services, e.g. Jenkins, Argo, Spinnaker Observability tools, e.g. Prometheus, Grafana, Splunk, ElasticSearch, Argus Track record of architecting and operating systems at planet scale, with demonstrated ownership of major design decisions General Skills Proven experience designing and operating APIs and async workflows at scale Deep experience with infrastructure as code AI proficiency, including applying AI tools to accelerate design, development, and operations Strong grasp of DevOps principles and how to instill them across a team Demonstrated ability to lead multiple concurrent initiatives, set technical priorities, and adapt plans as needs shift Excellent technical leadership, mentorship, and communication skills, with the ability to influence architecture decisions across teams and drive consensus among senior stakeholders Team Description Are you passionate about operating infrastructure at massive scale and being part of the team that builds the backbone of cloud services?
