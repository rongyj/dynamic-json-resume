## Summary

Foundation Health builds an AI operating system for pharmacy that streamlines care coordination and reduces administrative work for pharmacy teams. The Principal Software Engineer will serve as the most senior individual contributor in the US organization, owning architecture across product areas, building scalable systems, and solving complex applied AI, EHR integration, and platform engineering problems hands-on.

## Core responsibilities

- Agentic AI in a regulated environment: voice and SMS agents interacting with real patients, with the evals, guardrails and pharmacist-oversight architecture that makes that safe. This is the hard version of applied AI — the version where "it hallucinated" isn't a funny screenshot
- Deep EHR integration: Epic queues, prior authorization automation, 340B compliance — systems where correctness genuinely matters and the specs were written before you graduated
- Target-state architecture for the platform as it scales from a handful of health systems to hundreds — and building the first mile of the migration yourself
- Our AI engineering itself: model selection, context management, cost/latency tradeoffs, evaluation methodology
