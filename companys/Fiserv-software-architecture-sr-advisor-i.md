## Summary

Fiserv is a global fintech and payments company that provides secure, scalable financial technology solutions. The Staff Software Engineer – Event Messaging will provide technical leadership in designing, developing, and evolving Finxact’s event-driven platform, messaging capabilities, and distributed systems using Golang, Apache Kafka, Kubernetes, REST, gRPC, and cloud-native technologies.

## Core responsibilities

- Design, develop, and evolve highly scalable, resilient, and maintainable event-driven services and messaging platform capabilities
- Provide technical leadership and architectural guidance for distributed systems and event-driven application architectures
- Develop production services and platform components using Golang
- Design and implement scalable event streaming and messaging solutions using Apache Kafka
- Define architectural patterns and engineering standards for producers, consumers, topics, schemas, event contracts, retries, error handling, and message processing
- Design solutions that account for ordering, delivery guarantees, idempotency, backpressure, failure recovery, and distributed-system failure scenarios
- Develop APIs and supporting services using REST and gRPC where appropriate
- Collaborate with application, platform, database, and architecture teams to integrate event-driven capabilities across the Finxact platform
- Design, deploy, and operate containerized services within Kubernetes environments
- Develop and improve CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation to support reliable and repeatable delivery of messaging services
- Analyze performance, scalability, and reliability characteristics of event-driven systems and identify opportunities for improvement
- Troubleshoot complex production issues involving event processing, service communication, Kafka, databases, and distributed systems
- Establish observability practices for messaging platforms, including metrics, logging, tracing, lag monitoring, and operational alerting
- Participate in architecture reviews, technical design sessions, code reviews, and performance analysis
- Mentor engineers and provide technical guidance on Golang, Kafka, distributed systems, event-driven architecture, and cloud-native development
- Influence technical direction across teams and drive consistency in messaging architecture and engineering practices
