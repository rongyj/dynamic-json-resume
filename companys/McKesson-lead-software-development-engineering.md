## Summary

McKesson is an impact-driven healthcare company delivering insights, products, and services that make quality care more accessible and affordable. The Lead Software Engineer provides technical leadership and architecture guidance for enterprise oncology technology platforms, designing secure, scalable, cloud-native systems and integrations.
