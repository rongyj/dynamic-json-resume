## Summary

Accelerate Learning provides STEM curriculum and professional development solutions that empower teachers and inspire students through innovative instructional technology. The Software Architect owns the end-to-end architecture, operability, and evolution of core curriculum and supplemental product platforms, including production systems, cloud-native infrastructure, AI capabilities, and non-functional requirements. The role also contributes hands-on to engineering delivery, modernization, technical governance, and mentorship.

## Core responsibilities

- Serve as Software Architect for assigned core curriculum and supplemental applications/Services, holding end-to-end accountability for architectural integrity across both existing production micro services and new capabilities
- Own the maintenance and operability of assigned production applications—ensuring systems remain healthy, supportable, and fully operational through disciplined incident management, dependency tracking, runbook maintenance, and proactive operational risk reduction
- Own the ongoing architectural health of production systems—monitoring technical debt, identifying structural risk, and driving remediation plans that balance modernization with business continuity
- Lead application maintainability efforts—incremental re-architecture, strangler fig migrations, and system consolidations assuring minimal disruption to live customers across core curriculum and supplemental product lines
- Partner with Product, Agile Delivery and Engineering leadership to maintain a platform health backlog—making the cost and risk of deferred maintenance, architectural debt, and operability gaps visible and actionable at the team and leadership level
- Review and approve architectural changes to production systems, including database schema migrations, infrastructure topology changes, and API contract modifications, with a bias toward operability and rollback safety
- Ensure existing integrations (LMS/LTI, identity providers, content delivery, assessment platforms) remain secure, performant, and compliant with district and regulatory requirements (FERPA, COPPA)
- Act as the explicit owner of Non-Functional Requirements for assigned platforms—defining, measuring, and driving accountability for performance, scalability, reliability, security, and maintainability as first-class deliverables alongside feature work
- Define performance and scalability targets in partnership with engineering and QA; oversee performance testing strategies (load testing, chaos engineering) to validate production readiness ahead of district onboarding cycles and school-year peaks
- Establish observability standards: metrics, distributed tracing, structured logging, and alerting baselines that give teams early warning of degradation in existing systems
- Operate as an active delivery participant—not solely an advisor. Contribute hands-on to proof-of-concept builds, critical path engineering work, and architectural spikes alongside the team
- Collaborate closely with Tech Leads on sprint-level design decisions, ensuring architectural intent translates into executable, well-structured implementation
- Participate in code and design reviews to enforce standards, catch structural drift early, and use review cycles as a teaching and alignment mechanism
- Define the future-state product architecture for core curriculum and supplemental platforms, ensuring scalability, performance, and innovation across all products
- Drive adoption of modern cloud-native architectures on AWS—event-driven, microservices, and Kubernetes-based patterns—appropriate to each product context
- Design and evolve AI systems that support student tutoring, instructional assistance, and teacher insights features within core curriculum and supplemental products
- Lead technical due diligence for new tools, frameworks, and vendor platforms, evaluating fit against existing architecture and operability standards before adoption
- Mentor senior engineers, raising the technical bar and cultivating a culture of engineering excellence
- Provide architectural oversight on critical initiatives such as AI/ML integration, advanced analytics, platform interoperability, and content management strategy
