## Summary

Skimmer is a company modernizing the pool and spa service, repair, and construction industry through its pool service platform. The Senior DevOps Engineer will build and own the infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, deployment workflows, monitoring, and security for a new product line while partnering with development, QA, and Product teams.

- Stand up monitoring and alerting across applications and databases
- Use metrics and telemetry to proactively identify performance issues and drive improvements
- Partner with engineering teams to ensure applications are observable and secure

- Prioritize technical improvements that enhance uptime, performance, and observability
