CareSource is seeking an Architect III to partner with internal IT teams on critical Cloud, DevOps, and Site Reliability Engineering initiatives supporting Digital Solutions delivery teams. The role will design cloud architectures, infrastructure tooling, deployment strategies, DevOps automations, and SRE practices to improve the scalability, reliability, and performance of the Digital platform.
