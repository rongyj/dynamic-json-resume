Salt AI is building a compliance-first intelligence and orchestration platform for regulated life sciences organizations. The Senior DevOps Platform Engineer will own the deployment backbone for the company’s multi-environment AI platform, designing and operating reliable, secure infrastructure across SaaS, VPC, and on-premises environments. The role focuses on CI/CD, cloud consistency, observability, automation, security, reliability, and incident response.
