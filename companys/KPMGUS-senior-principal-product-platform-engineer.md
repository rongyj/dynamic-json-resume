KPMG is building a product organization designed to help transform decades of industry experience into innovative, software-powered businesses. Backed by the scale, resources, and market presence of a global network, this organization operates with the energy, ambition, and pace of a startup. We are assembling a founding team of builders, innovators, and problem solvers who are excited by the opportunity to create new products, shape new ways of working, and help define the future of technology-enabled business at KPMG. KPMG is currently seeking a Senior Principal Product & Platform Engineer to join our Product organization. Responsibilities: Develop and evolve the shared platform architecture frameworks and IT platforms that align technology infrastructure with business processes and data including, integration patterns, data and identity models, AI or agent frameworks, and reference architectures product teams build against Assess existing capabilities for architecture quality, reusability, integration feasibility, security posture, technical debt, and cost to convert them to shared platform assets, while researching emerging technologies; provide technical input for build, buy, partner, or harvest decisions to product leaders Develop and maintain reusable architecture standards and an AI-native prototyping framework that enables teams to move rapidly from product ideas to working prototypes Serve as the senior technical voice embedded within Product Management, connecting product evaluation with architecture, engineering, security, integration, and long-term platform health Create or guide prototypes for early product ideas, partnering with product management and client-facing leaders, that test technical feasibility and prevent one-off bespoke solutions Serve as the senior technical authority within Product Management, guiding architecture decisions, sharing leading practices, and coaching professionals across product and engineering teams Act with integrity, professionalism, and personal responsibility to uphold KPMG's respectful and courteous work environment Qualifications: Minimum twelve years of recent engineering experience on a principal or distinguished individual contributor track Bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university is preferred;
